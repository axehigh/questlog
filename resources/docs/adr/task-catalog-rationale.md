Splitting chores into small, bite-sized tasks makes them far less overwhelming for a 14-year-old while creating more frequent opportunities for quick "dopamine hits" through XP and coin popups.

Organizing your list into logical categories with a clear point hierarchy creates a balanced, engaging structure:

---

### 1. Daily Personal Space & Micro-Habits (Low-Barrier / Fast Completion)

These keep the personal space functional and build momentum.

* **Tidy Floor:** Pick up clothes, bags, and items so floor space is clear.
* **Tidy Desk:** Clear plates, trash, and organize cable/workspace.
* **Vacuum Own Room:** Quick pass once floor is clear.
* **Pet Care (Cat):** Refill food & refill fresh water (can be split or bundled as a quick daily check).

---

### 2. Family Space Chores (Shared Responsibility / Mid-Tier Rewards)

House-wide tasks carry a slightly higher reward because they contribute to the whole household.

* **Vacuum Stairs**
* **Vacuum Downstairs Gaming Room**
* **Vacuum Downstairs TV Room**
* **Bake Bread:** Higher-effort task that earns a bigger chunk of coins/XP.

---

### 3. Personal Growth & Habit Stacking (Progression Bonuses)

Combining base tasks with scaled bonuses incentivizes extra effort and consistency.

* **Read Book (15 mins):** Baseline habit option (+25 XP / +25 Coins).
* **Read Book (30 mins):** **Non-linear scale reward.** Rewarding 30 minutes with more than double the 15-minute value (e.g., +65 XP / +65 Coins) encourages longer focus sessions.
* **Reading Streak Bonus (5 of 7 Days):** A weekly achievement payout unlocked once the weekly milestone is met, tapping into completionist drive.
* **Go to Training:** Attending sports/workout sessions.

---

### Recommended Point & Penalty Hierarchy

To maintain motivation without creating a point inflation problem, structure values relative to effort:

| Task | Type | Suggested XP / Coins | Missed Task Penalty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Refill Cat Food & Water** | Daily | +15 / +15 | -20 Coins |
| **Tidy Floor / Tidy Desk** | Daily | +20 / +20 | -20 Coins |
| **Read Book (15 min)** | Habit | +25 / +25 | None (Optional) |
| **Read Book (30 min)** | Habit | +65 / +65 *(Scaled Up)* | None (Optional) |
| **Vacuum Own Room** | Weekly/Daily | +40 / +40 | -30 Coins |
| **Vacuum Stairs / TV Room / Gaming Room** | Area Task | +50 / +50 (each) | -30 Coins |
| **Go to Training** | Habit | +75 / +75 | -50 Coins |
| **Bake Bread** | Skill / Quest | +100 / +100 | None |
| **Reading Streak (5/7 Days)** | Weekly Quest | +250 Bonus / +250 Bonus | None |
